### Background

Darrow grew up inside a Horizon executive family. He had a penthouse, the VIP list at every club in Seattle, a corporate SIN, and designer bioware paid for by his father. He has always been able to talk his way into or out of anything, and he never had to work for it.

Then his parents cut him off. Maybe it was the novacoke, or the scandal, or one favor too many. The trust fund is gone, and the penthouse lease runs out at the end of the month. He still has expensive tastes and a habit to feed. The only skill he has is making people want to say yes. So now he does that for money, as the voice of a shadowrunner crew.

He is not a fighter. When guns come out, he gets small, finds cover, and keeps talking.

## How dice work (the 60-second version)

- **Dice pool.** Most rolls are an **attribute + a skill**. You roll that many six-sided dice.
- **Hits.** Every 5 or 6 is a **hit**. You need enough hits to beat a set number (the threshold) or the other side's hits (an opposed test).
- **Limit.** A **limit** caps how many hits count. Social rolls use your **Social limit**, physical rolls your **Physical limit**, and thinking or noticing rolls your **Mental limit**. A weapon's Accuracy is the limit for shooting it.
- **Specialization.** A specialization adds **+2 dice** when it applies.
- **Glitch.** If more than half your dice come up 1, you **glitch** and something goes wrong, even if you succeed.
- **Wounds.** Every 3 boxes of damage you've taken gives −1 to all your rolls.

Darrow's limits:

| Limit | Value | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Social** | **11** | All talking skills. 11 is very high, so most of his hits will count. |
| Mental | 5 | Perception, knowledge skills, First Aid, computers |
| Physical | 4 | Sneaking, Palming, running, climbing |

## Qualities

### Exceptional Attribute (Charisma), positive, 14 karma (p.72)
This raises his Charisma maximum from 6 to 7. He was born to charm people.
- **In play:** nothing to remember. It's built into his Charisma 7.

### First Impression, positive, 11 karma (p.74)
He gets **+2 dice on all social skills the first time he meets someone.**
- **Example:** at the first meeting with a Mr. Johnson, he gets +2 to negotiate the fee. At the second meeting, Johnson already has an opinion of him, so no bonus.
- **Example:** walking up to a club bouncer he's never seen, he gets +2 on Con to get in without being on the list.
- Remind the GM about this one. It's easy to forget.

### SINner (Corporate Limited): Horizon, negative, gives him 15 karma (p.84)
His real identity: a corporate SIN registered with Horizon. It's real, so it holds up to any check. It's also traceable. If a crime is ever linked to Darrow Vance, Horizon and the police know exactly who to look for, and his family's name is involved.
- **In play:** **never use his real SIN on a run.** Use Darren Vale.
- **Story hook:** Horizon may decide that their disgraced heir is useful to them, or a liability.

### Addiction (Moderate): Novacoke, negative, gives him 9 karma (p.77–78)
He's psychologically and physically hooked on novacoke, a stimulant. If he goes too long without a dose, or gets very stressed, the GM calls for an **addiction test (5 dice: Body + Willpower)**. Failing it means cravings and withdrawal, with penalties until he gets a fix.
- **Example:** the crew has been holed up in a safehouse for three days. The GM calls for a test, he fails, and he's at a penalty until he finds a way to use.
- **In play:** carry your novacoke, and treat Rico (your dealer) well.

## Body modifications

### Tailored Pheromones, Rating 3 (bioware, 0.6 Essence, 93,000¥, p.460)
Glands that release pheromones tuned to make people like and trust him. He gets **+3 dice on six skills when the target can smell him:**
- the **Influence** group: Etiquette, Leadership, Negotiation
- the **Acting** group: Con, Impersonation, Performance

They also add **+3 to his Social limit**, which is why it's 11.
- **Works:** face-to-face, in the same room, at the bar, across a table.
- **Doesn't work:** on a call, through a door, against someone in a gas mask or a sealed suit, against drones.
- **Doesn't help Intimidation.** Intimidation isn't in either group.

### Voice Modulator, Rating 4 (headware, 0.2 Essence, 20,000¥, p.452)
An implant in his throat. He can make his voice sound like anyone or anything, and it adds **+4 dice to Impersonation**.
- **Example:** he calls a security desk and sounds exactly like the night supervisor.
- **Example:** he sounds like a different person on every call, so his voice can't be matched.
- It's a **Forbidden** implant, and Forbidden items can never be licensed. If a cyberware scanner finds it, he's in trouble. See "What happens without a license" under Identity.

## Skills

### Talking: his job

| Skill | Pool | Up close (+3 pheromones) | + first meeting (+2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Con** (spec: Fast Talking) | 13 (15) | 16 (18) | 18 (20) |
| **Negotiation** | 12 | 15 | 17 |
| **Etiquette** | 12 | 15 | 17 |
| **Leadership** | 12 | 15 | 17 |
| **Impersonation** (voice modulator) | 15 | 18 | 20 |
| **Performance** | 10 | 13 | 15 |
| **Intimidation** (spec: Mental) | 12 (14) | n/a, no pheromones | 14 (16) |
| **Disguise** | 7 | n/a | n/a |

- **Con:** lying, bluffing, misdirection. It's for the *short* lie; a long-term cover identity is Impersonation.
  - *"I'm with building maintenance, the elevator in 4B is down."*
  - Convincing a guard that the crew's van belongs at the loading dock.
  - **Fast Talking (+2)** applies when he's improvising, with no time to prepare: talking his way out of a checkpoint he didn't expect.
- **Negotiation:** making deals. He uses it to raise the pay on a run, buy gear cheaper, sell loot for more, and work out terms between two sides.
  - *Johnson offers 5,000¥ each. Darrow rolls Negotiation against Johnson, and every net hit pushes the fee up.*
- **Etiquette:** fitting in and not making mistakes in a social setting: a corporate gala, a gang bar, a yakuza teahouse.
  - Getting into an exclusive party and moving around it without anyone wondering who he is.
  - Knowing not to look the oyabun in the eye.
- **Leadership:** getting people to follow, calm down or keep going.
  - Calming a standoff: *"Everybody lower your weapons, nobody gets paid if we're all dead."*
  - Steadying a panicking hostage so they don't run into the line of fire.
  - Rallying a scared teammate.
- **Intimidation:** threats, open or implied. **Mental (+2)** covers psychological pressure, as opposed to violence.
  - *"I know where your sister goes to school"* to make a wage slave hand over a keycard.
  - Interrogating a captured guard by working on their fears.
  - Pheromones don't help here. Someone who likes you is harder to scare.
- **Impersonation:** being someone else, either a specific person or a type ("a Lone Star detective").
  - With the voice modulator, he calls the target's assistant pretending to be the boss.
  - Posing as a building inspector for a whole afternoon.
- **Performance:** acting, singing, public speaking, putting on a show.
  - A distraction: a drunk, loud scene in the lobby while the crew slips past.
  - Giving a speech at a corporate event to keep everyone's eyes on him.
- **Disguise (Intuition):** changing how he looks: makeup, wigs, clothes. It works with Impersonation. He's not great at it.

**How licenses get checked.** A license is tied to one SIN. When someone checks Darren Vale's licenses, they check the SIN too, so a failed check on either one can burn both. The licenses are rating 3 and the SIN is rating 4, so the licenses are the weaker link.

### What happens without a license

| | **Restricted** ("R") | **Forbidden** ("F") |
|---|---|---|
| **Examples on Darrow** | Pistol, pheromones, bug scanner, tag eraser, drugs, ammo | **Voice modulator** |
| **Can it be licensed?** | Yes | No, never |
| **If caught without a license** | Usually: the item is confiscated, a fine, a flag on the SIN he showed, and some hard questions. Worse if it ties into a crime. | Arrest-level trouble. It's evidence that he's a criminal, and it burns the identity he was using. |

How likely he is to get caught depends on what gets scanned:
- **Weapons** show up on the scanners at checkpoints, building lobbies and clubs. The concealed-carry license covers the pistol.
- **Cyberware** (the voice modulator) shows up on **cyberware scanners** at airports, corp facilities and police stops. This is his real exposure. Keep away from places that scan for implants, or have a story ready.
- **Bioware** (the pheromones) is grown tissue, so ordinary cyberware scanners can't see it. It takes a bioscanner or a medical exam. The license is mostly for when someone does look closely.
- **Gear** like the bug scanner and the drugs comes up when he, his bag or his car is searched.

How strict any of this is depends on the GM.

## How to play Darrow

**Before the meeting:**
- Ask the decker or fixer for the Johnson's name, and look them up with **High Society** or **Corporate Politics**.
- Sweep the room with the **bug scanner** and turn on the **white noise generator**.

**At the table:**
- Tell the GM what you want and *how* you say it. Then roll the skill that fits:
  - **Con** to lie
  - **Negotiation** to make a deal
  - **Etiquette** to fit in
  - **Leadership** to calm or rally people
  - **Intimidation** to threaten
- Remind the GM: **+3 pheromones** if they can smell you, **+2 First Impression** on a first meeting.
- Use **Judge Intentions (12 dice)** whenever you think someone's lying to you.
- For the roll that really matters, spend **Edge: Push the Limit**.

**When it goes wrong:**
1. **Talk first:** Leadership to calm people down.
2. **Take cover second.**
3. **Shoot third:** gel rounds if you can, regular rounds if you must. Pistols, 10 dice.

He has 10 Physical and 9 Stun boxes and armor 8. He can take a hit or two, not a firefight.

**The habit:**
- Carry your novacoke.
- When the GM calls an addiction test, roll 5 dice (Body + Willpower).
- If you fail, play the cravings. It's good drama.

**Never** use his real Horizon SIN on a job.
