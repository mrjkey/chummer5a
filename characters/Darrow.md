**Status:** complete and legal. Chummer's finish-creation check reports no errors and no warnings. All 25 build karma (+24 from negative qualities) is spent. 915¥ is left as pocket money; up to 5,000¥ may carry over.

## Concept

Darrow is a nepo baby. He grew up inside a Horizon executive family: penthouse, club VIP lists, a corporate SIN, designer 'ware paid for by daddy. He has always been able to talk his way into or out of anything.

Then his parents cut him off. The trust fund is gone, the penthouse lease runs out next month, and he still has expensive tastes and a novacoke habit to feed. So now he talks for a living, on a shadowrunner crew.

He is the crew's voice. He haggles, sells, lies, charms, intimidates, and calms a standoff down before anyone pulls a trigger. When it does turn violent, he gets small, stays behind cover, and keeps talking.

## How to play Darrow (new players)

- **Talk first.** Most of your useful rolls are Charisma plus a social skill. Tell the GM what you want and how you are saying it, and roll Con (lies), Negotiation (deals), Etiquette (fitting in), Leadership (rallying or calming), or Intimidation (threats).
- **Your Social limit is 10.** It caps how many hits count on social tests, and 10 is very high.
- **Pheromones only work up close,** when people can smell you. On a call, you lose those 3 dice.
- **Edge is your luck.** Spend Edge points to reroll failures or add dice when a roll really matters. Lucky raised the cap.
- **In a fight:** stay behind cover, shoot gel rounds if you can't talk it down, and let the crew's shooter handle the rest.
- **The addiction:** carry your novacoke. Going too long without it, or being under stress, can mean withdrawal penalties. The GM will tell you when.
