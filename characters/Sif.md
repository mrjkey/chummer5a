## Concept

Sif grew up in the Redmond Barrens and learned to fight before they understood their magic. They react before someone pulls a trigger, and land shots other people call impossible. That is magic, running through their body instead of being cast as spells.

**Personality: the cocky hotshot, with a protector's soft spot.** Sif is charming and competitive, and convinced nobody in Seattle draws faster. They want money and a reputation. Under the swagger, every spare nuyen goes to Mama Lucia's noodle bar: the place that fed them as a kid, and that the local gang now wants protection money from. That is their own reason for needing the cash.

**Team role:** armed protection, covering an escape, handling threats. **Outside combat:** sneaking into places (lockpicks, Catlike) and working street contacts (Street Etiquette, gang knowledge).

## About "magically enhanced bullets"

The core rulebook has **no magic that enchants bullets.** Spells don't attach to ammunition, and weapon foci only work in melee. Later books add more options, but they are outside the core rules. So the build splits the idea in two:

1. **The magic is in the shooter.**
   - *Improved Reflexes:* reacts faster than anyone and gets extra initiative dice.
   - *Improved Ability (Pistols):* better aim.
   - *Enhanced Accuracy (Pistols):* the gun is more precise in their hands.
   - *Combat Sense:* better dodging.
   - *Danger Sense:* senses ambushes.

   This is how SR5 does a "magic gunslinger".
2. **The situational effects are in the magazine.** Sif carries six kinds of ammo and swaps clips depending on the threat:

| Ammo | Effect | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **APDS** (armor-piercing) | Ignores a lot of armor | Corp security, armored targets |
| **Explosive rounds** | More damage | Something big needs to go down |
| **Hollow points** | More damage, worse against armor | Unarmored gangers |
| **Gel rounds** | Stun damage, knocks people back | Non-lethal: bouncers, innocents in the way |
| **Stick-n-Shock** | Electric stun damage that can leave a target stunned | Taking someone alive |
| **Regular** | Plain rounds | Everything else |

The quick-draw holster and four spare clips make swapping fast. At the table, describe it however you like: Sif "charging" a clip with a whisper is fine flavor. The rules effect is the ammo type. If the group later adds the *Street Grimoire* book, there are more magical options to explore.

## How to play Sif (new players)

- **Your main roll is Pistols:** 14 dice, 16 with the Predator thanks to the specialization. Hits are capped by the gun's accuracy, which the smartlink and Enhanced Accuracy raise.
- **Initiative 12 + 3d6** means you usually act first and get extra turns. Use them to take the dangerous enemy out, or to get your people to cover.
- **Choose your ammo before the shooting starts** (see the table above). Swapping a clip takes an action.
- **Dodge with Combat Sense:** you roll 3 extra dice when someone attacks you.
- **Out of bullets or too close?** Punch: Unarmed Combat with Critical Strike.
- **Sneaking pool 12:** you can get the crew in quietly, too.
- **Edge is your luck.** Spend Edge to reroll a missed shot that really matters.
